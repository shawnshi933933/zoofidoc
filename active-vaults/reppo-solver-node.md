# Reppo Solver Node

## About Reppo

Reppo's mission is to build a decentralized version of Scale AI on-chain where everyone involved in the generation and monetization of AI training data shares the upside, without intermediaries involved.

## Calculation of aVT（Accrued Vesting Token）

Reppo Solver Nodes are divided into two types: Standard Node and Premium Node. According to Reppo's documentation, the amount of tokens they can earn is:

* 1 Standard Node can earn 2555.6 $REPPO  — aVT= 2555.6
* 1 Premium Node can earn 7202.2 $REPPO — aVT = 7207.2
